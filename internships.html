What is a spring internship?

A spring internship (or "spring week") is a 1–2 week programme run by banks and financial firms, usually in March or April. It is the first step into the industry: many firms use it to fast-track students to a summer internship, and summer internships lead to graduate job offers.

Most spring weeks are open to first-year university students, and applications open from around September to January. Firms often review applications as they arrive, so applying early matters. Before university, you can build your CV and apply to insight days and school-level programmes that some firms run.
