How to use it

  1. Create a free account and turn on alerts, so you're told the day a programme opens.
  2. Pick 8–15 firms to target. Quality beats quantity: each application needs its own research.
  3. Add each one to My applications (next tab) with its deadline.
  4. Build a company profile for each firm before you write the application.

Apply early. Many firms review applications as they arrive and close once they have enough candidates, sometimes before the official deadline. Aim to apply within 1–2 weeks of a programme opening.
